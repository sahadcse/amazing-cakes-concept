Flavors & Combinations

Every Flavor, Every Filling, Every Favorite — With the Photos to Match.

The bakery lists 11 cake flavors and 24 fillings, and its cupcake menu already pairs many of them together. Below: those pairings shown as the bakery photographs them, the complete lists, and the seven combinations the bakery itself calls favorites. Any pairing beyond those is a possibility to discuss — not a published promise.

Published pairings

Flavors + Fillings, as the Bakery Combines Them.

Each of the bakery’s cupcakes is a working example of its flavor-and-filling combinations — names, descriptions and prices exactly as published ($3.50 each).

The full list

All 11 Cake Flavors.

The flavors published for custom cakes. Custom flavors and combinations are confirmed directly with the bakery at inquiry — as the bakery puts it, its online selection is limited, but it has “endless possibilities in store.”

Pair any of these with the fillings below — see cake sizes & servings for how much each size serves.

Fillings

All 24 Fillings, in Families.

Grouped so the list is easy to scan — creamy, fruity, chocolatey or something in between.

The 11 flavors and 24 fillings can be discussed in many pairings — only the combinations below are published by the bakery as favorites, and any specific combination is confirmed at inquiry.

Published pairings

Bakery Favorites.

Seven combinations the bakery itself publishes. Three of them appear word-for-word on the cupcake menu — the photographs are those actual items.

    The other four favorites are published pairings without cupcake photos — Marble + Hazelnut Mocha, Strawberry + Bavarian Mousse, Funfetti + Raspberry Mousse, and Lemon + Lemon Raspberry.
